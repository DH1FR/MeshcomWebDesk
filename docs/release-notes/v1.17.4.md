# Release Notes – v1.17.4

## What is new in v1.17.4

**Donations via Wero or bank transfer, and a Windows setup in five languages.**

---

## ☕ Wero and bank transfer

- The coffee dialog, the **About** page and the README now point out that a donation also works via
  **Wero** or **SEPA bank transfer**.
- A short e-mail with a prefilled subject is enough – you get the bank details back, or the amount is
  requested via Wero. If you would like a license, add your callsign.
- Texts in German, English, Spanish, French and Italian.

---

## 🪟 Windows setup

- All setup texts (tasks, shortcuts, status messages, service description) are translated into
  **German, English, Spanish, French and Italian**.
- **Spanish, French and Italian** are new wizard languages next to German and English.
- The setup script now has a UTF-8 BOM, so umlauts and accents are shown correctly.

---

## 🇮🇹 Translation

- Italian texts improved – thanks to **giovanni69** ([#30](https://github.com/DH1FR/MeshcomWebDesk/pull/30)).
